Tidy for Safari

Support

A quick path from a stubborn website back to a clean browsing session.

Enable Tidy

  1. Open Safari and choose Manage Extensions from the Page Menu.
  2. Turn on Tidy.
  3. Allow access to all websites. Tidy needs this permission only to inspect and clean the site you choose.
  4. Open Tidy from the Page Menu whenever a website needs attention.

If data remains

Safari intentionally keeps some website data unavailable to extensions. Tidy reports those boundaries instead of claiming a complete deletion. Safari history, saved passwords, and some HttpOnly cookies may remain.

If cleanup signs you out

Use the site's normal sign-in flow again. For less disruptive cleanup, choose the tracking-only recommendation rather than resetting all accessible site data.

Contact

For help, email hrish2006@gmail.com. Include your iOS version, the website hostname, and what Tidy reported. Do not send cookie or storage values.
