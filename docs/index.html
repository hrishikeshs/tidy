Tidy for Safari

Website cleanup,
kept on your device.

Tidy helps you inspect and remove accessible website state in Safari without an account, analytics, or a developer-operated backend.

Private by construction

Tidy's catalog, classifications, learned policies, and cleanup history stay on your iPhone or iPad. The app does not send them to its developer or to a third-party analytics service.
