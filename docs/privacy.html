Tidy for Safari

Privacy policy

Effective September 11, 2026. Tidy does not collect your data or transmit it to the developer.

What stays on your device

The Safari extension keeps a local catalog containing observed website origins and hostnames, timestamps, category counts, inaccessible-category flags, and cleanup outcomes. It does not persist cookie values, website-storage values, URL paths, or detailed error text. Names shown during an on-demand inspection are not added to this catalog.

Learning Clean

Learning Clean uses a separate WebKit profile inside Tidy. When you choose to capture a page, it reads that profile's cookies and Web Storage values into memory and copies candidate state into temporary, non-persistent browser sessions on the device. The separate profile itself may retain the website's ordinary data so you can return to it. Saved cleanup policies contain names, scopes, outcomes, and timestamps—but never values—and expire after 14 days.

Website access

Tidy asks Safari for access to websites because inspection and cleanup are its core functions. Safari controls this permission. Tidy uses it to enumerate and remove user-selected website state that Safari makes available. Tidy does not sell, share, or send this information to the developer.

Network activity

Tidy has no developer-operated backend, account system, analytics service, advertising SDK, or cloud sync. When you open or clean a website, Safari or Tidy's isolated browser loads that website; the website and resources it chooses may receive ordinary web requests under their own privacy policies. Opening the support, privacy, or acknowledgements links loads this static website.

Optional tips

Tidy offers optional one-time tips through Apple's In-App Purchase system. Tips unlock no features and do not change how Tidy works. Apple processes the purchase under its own privacy policy; Tidy does not receive or store your payment details.

Removing local information

You can remove accessible site data from Tidy's Safari workspace. The clear-all action may sign you out and reset site preferences. Deleting Tidy removes the app's own local data according to iOS behavior. Safari may retain website data that extensions cannot access, as well as history and saved passwords.

Children

Tidy is a general-purpose Safari utility and is not directed to children. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.
