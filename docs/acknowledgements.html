Open Cookie Database

Tidy includes a normalized, generated subset of the Open Cookie Database, licensed under the Apache License 2.0.

The included snapshot contains 2,261 retained rules. Records are flattened, category names are compacted, domain-like tokens are normalized, descriptions are whitespace-normalized and truncated, duplicates are removed, and unsafe context-free names shorter than three characters are excluded.
